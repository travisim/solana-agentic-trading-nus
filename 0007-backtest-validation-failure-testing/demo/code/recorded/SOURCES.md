## What is simulated tonight (and labelled so)

- The faults (stale oracle, RPC outage, slippage shock, fee change, duplicate submission) are SIMULATED on top of the real prices by `src/faults.ts`. The moves they inject (for example an 80 bps market move) are chosen by the code, not observed. No live service is attacked and nothing is sent anywhere.
- The paper agent (one 250 USD order every 30 s) is a test harness, not a strategy.
- The 50 random strategies per draw are generated by `src/factory.ts` from a seed.

## Limits (read before drawing conclusions)

- 40 minutes on one quiet night is tiny. About 480 samples, 5 to 80 trades per strategy, so every score here has a wide error bar. Differences of a few points in the accuracy table are noise (one standard error is about 2.4 points at 433 test moments).
- Before costs, some random strategies make a little money; after the 5 bps fee and 2 bps slippage per leg that build night 1 used, none of the 50 strategies in draw 1 makes money in-sample (their winners are all at or below zero). That is a fact about this window, not about markets.
- Nothing here is a trading result. "Simulated, on recorded data; not advice."
